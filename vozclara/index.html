Straight answers

The questions you are already asking.

Does my crew have to learn an app?

No. They tap one button and talk, in Spanish, the way they already speak. That is the whole point. If they can leave a voicemail, they can use VozClara.

Is my jobsite data going to some cloud?

No. The translation runs on a local model on your machine. Nothing is shipped off to a third party and there are no per-word fees that climb with use.

What if it hears something wrong?

The crew member sees a plain-Spanish summary of what was understood and confirms it before it reaches your log. When something is unclear, it asks rather than guesses.
