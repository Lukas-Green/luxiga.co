How I Work

Services

I listen for where the work actually hurts, scope the smallest tool that fixes it, and build it local-first so it gets adopted and stays adopted.

01

AI Consulting

Most AI advice is noise. I find the one place in your operation where it actually pays for itself, and I am honest about the ten places it does not. You leave with a tool that still works six months after it ships, not the demo that wowed the room and got abandoned.

02

Systems Automation

Recurring manual work is a design failure, not a fact of life. I map the exact repetition and engineer it away. What I build is lightweight, maintainable, and invisible by design. Your team should forget it is there and just feel the day get shorter.

03

Design for Adoption

Capability means nothing if no one uses it. Two decades of design thinking go into every build, so the interface matches how people actually work, not how an engineer wishes they would. If someone needs a walkthrough to use it, it’s not done yet.

04

Quality Engineering

Shipping fast counts for nothing if it breaks in front of your customer. I interview your team to find exactly where quality slips through, then build the safety net: real test coverage, release gates, and a process people actually follow. I can stand QA up from scratch, fix a pipeline that fights you, or onboard your team so the quality outlasts me.

05

Guided Process Design

Some processes are only hard because nobody ever wrote them down properly. Financial aid, licensing, onboarding, a degree audit, a vendor setup that assumes you already know the answer. I read the whole sprawling mess against the rules that actually govern it, then rebuild it as a path with one clear next step. Written at three levels of detail, so a nervous first-timer and someone who has done it twice can work from the same document. Nobody should need a second job to finish a form.
