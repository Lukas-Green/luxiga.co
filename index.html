01Services

Five ways in. Each one starts with listening.

I listen for where the work actually hurts, scope the smallest tool that fixes it, and build it local-first so it gets adopted and stays adopted.

01AI ConsultingFind the one place AI pays for itself, and hear plainly where it does not.

I look for the one place in your operation where AI actually earns its keep, and I tell you honestly about the places it would not. You leave with a tool that still works six months after it ships, not a demo that gets abandoned.

02Systems AutomationMap the repeat work, then build it out of your week.

If your team does the same manual steps every week, I map exactly where the repetition is and build a lightweight tool that takes it over. It should be easy to maintain and easy to forget about. Your team just notices the day getting shorter.

03Design for AdoptionTools shaped around how your people already work.

A tool nobody uses gives nothing back. Two decades of design go into every build, so the interface fits how people actually work. If someone needs a walkthrough to use it, it is not done yet.

04Quality EngineeringTest coverage and release gates your team will actually follow.

I talk with your team to find where quality slips through, then build the safety net: real test coverage, release gates, and a process people follow. I can set QA up from scratch, fix a pipeline that fights you, or train your team so the quality outlasts me.

05Guided Process DesignTurn a confusing process into one clear next step at a time.

Financial aid, licensing, onboarding, a degree audit, a vendor setup that assumes you already know the answer. I read the whole thing against the rules that actually govern it, then rebuild it as a path with one clear next step. It is written at three levels of detail, so a nervous first-timer and someone who has done it twice can work from the same document.Independent work, not affiliated with or endorsed by any school, agency or lender.

05About

A designer who learned to build. Then everything clicked.

Lukas Green, founder

LUXIGA is my studio. I work with small teams and mission-driven organizations on the places where AI can take friction out of the work and make systems easier to use.

I build automation tools, internal platforms and AI-assisted workflows, and every project starts from one specific problem someone is tired of. When AI tools arrived and I realized I could build what I designed, not just design it, everything clicked.

Before that I came up through design, doing in-house print and design work from 2004 onward. Two decades of developing an eye for what works across print, digital and UX. I am finishing my degree at Portland State.
