The Frame

Buy a template. Customize with AI. Ship.

Most vertical SaaS is built the wrong way around. The vendor designs a generic dashboard, then asks the customer to learn it, configure it, and reshape their business to fit. The customer becomes the architect of a tool they paid someone else to build.

The frame I'm building works in the opposite direction. Start with a pre-built architecture template designed from real market research into a specific vertical. When a customer adopts it, they go through an LLM-powered onboarding flow that asks the right questions for their industry: clinic size, EHR system, project types, estimating method. The AI maps those answers to the template's configuration and customizes the system to their exact workflow. Compliance checks, documentation, automations, dashboards. Hours instead of months.

"The gap isn't a lack of tools. It's that every tool requires the customer to become the architect. The frame flips that."
How It Works

Three steps from prospect to production

  • Step 1. Pick a vertical. Each template starts from a deep research brief: market size, competitive landscape, real pain points, automation opportunities. The vertical isn't picked from a catalog. It's picked because the research surfaces a real gap.
  • Step 2. AI onboarding. A guided wizard asks questions specific to the customer's industry and business. The LLM maps responses to template configuration: workflows, compliance checklists, documentation, automation rules. The customer never sees a blank dashboard.
  • Step 3. Deploy. The customized system is ready to use. Local AI agents handle the repetitive work (filing, scheduling, billing, field communication) while the customer focuses on the work that matters.
