Privacy

Updated October 13, 2026. LUXIGA LLC, Oregon.

The short version

This site has no analytics, no ad trackers, and sets no cookies of its own. It only collects what you choose to send, and it goes to Lukas Green and no one else.

What you send, and where it goes

  • Contact form. Your name, email, the scope you pick and your message. It is delivered through Formspree, a form-delivery service, to Lukas's inbox.
  • Pulse assessment. Your answers stay in your browser unless you choose to send them with your email. If you do, they go through Formspree to Lukas.
  • Business card page. The work-request form opens your own email app with the message filled in. Nothing is sent until you press send there.

Nothing you send is sold, shared for marketing, or added to a mailing list.

What stays in your browser

  • Your light or dark mode choice, if you use the toggle.
  • Anything you type into the demo tools. It is saved in your browser only so the demo works, and never leaves your device.

Outside services this site loads

  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces, so Google receives the usual web request from your browser.
  • Calendly handles booking. Booking links open Calendly's own site, under Calendly's privacy terms.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this site.

Deleting what you sent

Email lukas@luxiga.co and ask. Your messages and any copy of your Pulse answers will be deleted.
